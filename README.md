## Featured Engineering Projects

### 1. Self-Powered Autonomous Hydro-Valve System
**Discipline:** Electromechanical Systems, Renewable Power, Embedded IoT  
**Core Technologies:** In-Line Micro-Hydro Turbine, Heltec ESP32-S3 (LoRa 32 V4), TPS61088 Synchronous Boost, LR7843 MOSFET, SolidWorks  

* Engineered a 100% off-grid, maintenance-free automated 12V motorized ball valve system powered entirely by fluid flow kinetic energy, eliminating underground wiring and trenching.
* Implemented sub-GHz LoRa Channel Activity Detection (CAD) wake-up routines on an ESP32-S3, achieving microamp deep-sleep standby for subterranean soil penetration.
* Integrated dynamic MP1584EN buck regulation with 1N5819 Schottky diode reverse protection to maintain a permanent net-positive LiPo charge surplus.

### 2. 3D-Printed Functional Prosthetic Hand
**Discipline:** Mechanical Mechanisms, Biomechatronics, Additive Manufacturing  
**Core Technologies:** SolidWorks (Exploded Assemblies & Pin Clearances), Arduino Nano, Micro-Servo Actuation, Tendon Routing  

* Designed and fabricated an accessible, functional upper-limb prosthetic prototype with a material cost under $30 and total weight of 1.50 lbs.
* Engineered modular 3D-printed snap-pin joints and an offset anatomical thumb opposition angle, achieving a 1.0-second closing speed and 370 g payload grip.
* Modeled internal routing channels with chamfered entries in SolidWorks to minimize nylon tendon friction and line binding.
